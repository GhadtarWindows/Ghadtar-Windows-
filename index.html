Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

uPVC windows ki warranty kitni hoti hai?
Humari high-grade uPVC profiles aur manufacturing par long-lasting durability milti hai, jisme color fading ya warping ki koi problem nahi aati. Iski life 20-25 saal se zyada hoti hai.
Kya ye windows 100% soundproof hoti hain?
Haan! Double/triple glazing glass aur double-seal airtight mechanism ki wajah se yeh baahar ke shor-sharabe ko 90% tak rok deti hain.
Kya aap Palanpur ke alawa baaki cities mein bhi service dete hain?
Ji haan, hum Palanpur ke sath-sath Deesa, Danta, Abu Road, Unjha aur poore North Gujarat mein manufacturing, delivery aur installation ki service provide karte hain.
Order dene ke baad kitne din mein fitting ho jati hai?
Measurement lene ke baad aapke requirements ke mutabiq factory mein custom manufacturing hoti hai, aur aam taur par 7 se 10 dino ke andar installation complete kar di jati hai.
